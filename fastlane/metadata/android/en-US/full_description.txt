Senswear is an open-source, privacy-first universal wearable and health biometrics companion for Android.

Designed with an Apple Native Liquid Glass aesthetic and Material 3 Adaptive layouts, Senswear bridges raw physical Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) sensor streams with Google Health Connect and vendor cloud APIs—normalizing disparate biometric protocols into a single, verifiable domain model without cloud lock-in, advertising trackers, or synthetic data fabrication.

Key Capabilities:
• Universal Wearable Support: Direct BLE GATT Central support for Pebble Qore 2, Polar H10 / Verity Sense, Coros, Garmin HRM straps, Apple Watch broadcast, Samsung Galaxy Watch (Health Connect), and Google Pixel Watch.
• Liquid Glass Design System: Frosted glass cards with specular highlight edge refraction, obsidian foundations, and dynamic HUD status islands.
• 120 FPS Real-Time Cardiac Waveform: Hardware-accelerated ECG/PPG wave canvas synchronized to live pulse telemetry with zero recomposition overhead.
• Physiological Science Engine: Frequency-domain HRV (LF/HF autonomic balance), motion-artifact rejection filtering, circadian slow-wave sleep resting heart rate, and temperature baseline anomaly detection.
• Multi-Source Reconciliation: Linear clock skew estimation, authoritative sensor hierarchy arbitration, and automated sleep boundary stitching.
• 100% Privacy & Local Data Sovereignty: Zero analytics, zero advertising SDKs, encrypted on-device SQLite storage, biometric app lock, and single-click GPX/FIT/CSV data export.
